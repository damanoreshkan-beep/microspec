## env.hdr
Poly Haven — "Reinforced Concrete 01", 1K HDRI.
https://polyhaven.com/a/reinforced_concrete_01

Licence: **CC0** (https://polyhaven.com/license) — public domain dedication, no attribution required.
Recorded here anyway: provenance is what lets a future reader verify the licence instead of trusting it.

Chosen for 27 EV of dynamic range — one bright source in a dark concrete volume, which is the lighting the
hero scene reflects. Shipped as the original Radiance file and decoded by packages/runtime/hdr.js; the
renderer downsamples it at load, so there is no converted copy to keep in sync.
